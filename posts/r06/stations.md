建议优先集成 **cassette_fixed**：1450 × 750 × 1950 mm 独立备菜模块，单台固定安装 UR5e，不设移动轨道。机械臂主要搬运带定位把手的 GN 1/4 盆篮组件；模块自带水槽、冷水与排水接口、废料箱和一套家用安全防护。切削、削皮和去眼反力由机架承受。参考餐按六片菜叶编排为 **79 个上层事件，预计 45 分钟，预算 ¥268,985（含 15% 预备费）**。

**Recorded engineering decisions and remaining risks.** One fixed arm reduces rail hardware and eliminates simultaneity. A commercial multi-kilogram peeler is rejected for reference-sized loads and unknown depth; the compact Rotato is rejected as A's baseline because its140 mm food limit, pins, end scars and manual finishes increase transactions. CL20 is retained as B's real5 mm comparator, but no inlet fit is inferred from area. Standard solid outer pans catch tomato pulp and keep each ingredient separate. Gravity drainage replaces unnecessary spin hardware. The greens side output and closed-lid transport are deliberate state/event decisions. Custom machining is concentrated in one shared cell, with purchased replaceable cutting components; it remains a substantial development task.

Recommendation is **A for provisional integration, not build release**. The highest risks are irregular-root peel coverage/gouging, all-eye/core perception, low-force wet-leaf retention and sanitation, tomato skin/pulp preservation, active/inactive tool clearance, and cassette handoff geometry. The side port needs installed-room access. Time/BOM uncertainty remains material despite contingency. Failure or extra produce variation extends the recipe or yields an explicitly rejected bowl; it does not loosen food quality, cut geometry, force bounds or contact gates.
