### Displacement bound and motion coverage

The ≤4 mm step bound is valid for these compiled hinge/slide models. For every geometry and hinge ancestor, a global lever bound sums downstream fixed translations, pivot offsets, full permitted slide travel, geometry-center offset and enclosing radius. A slide contributes its absolute displacement. Triangle inequality gives `D_g = Σ R_gj |Δq_j| + Σ |Δslide|`; the maximum over both arms and all held geometries bounds every point. Opening-finger travel is included. A second bound uses the starting lever radius plus globally bounded downstream-only travel; the current hinge and upstream rigid motion preserve that radius. Taking the minimum of the two valid bounds is valid. This review reconstructs both bounds from the compiled model rather than trusting saved step fields.
