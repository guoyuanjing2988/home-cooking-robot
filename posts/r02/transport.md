推荐采用**两条独立并行轨道，每条一只机械臂，各自使用拖链**（`passing_rails_cable`）。机械臂留在各自轨道上，可以互相越过并在行程内任意位置停下；不用把机械臂本身搬过道岔。三臂运输部分预算为 **99,000 元**，两臂为 **74,000 元**，均不含机械臂、末端工具和加工电器，也不是供应商报价。备选是在每条轨道上增加 180 mm 的升降轴，三臂预算 **129,000 元**。真正的自由换轨在这间厨房里不值得新增供电、车载控制箱、锁止机构和清洁维护的成本。

本轮把轨道、拖链和支架都作为机器人实体检查。空夹爪保持安装，工具和食材先放回支撑物；两臂、三臂的静态折叠运输采样均满足环境间隙 ≥10 mm，最小值 12 mm，跨轨实体最小间隙 16.18 mm。现有番茄分瓣器必须旋转 90° 并移到 `(x,y)=(0.39,0.48)` m；不改变刀具或切割机构。三只完整协作臂会伸到对侧台面上方，不能让人同时使用过道，第三只臂的加工可达性也未成立。因此，三臂图和价格用于比较，不建议直接作为家用配置。

这是一份运输方案和仿真接口，**不是整机通过报告**。按最不利 25 kg 臂体、3 kg 食材、1.2 kg 夹爪及 1.5 动态系数，选用 60 kg / 900 N / 每轴 650 N·m 的运输设计包络。支撑梁每轨约 51 kg，墙面载荷很大，瓷砖、抹灰层、轻质砌块和吊柜柜体不能承担该方案。导轨和梁的初算有余量，但门架刚度、建筑锚固、原厂柔性线缆弯曲半径，以及机械臂重力轴弹簧制动和连续扭矩证据仍须闭合。

## Decision and scope

Date: 2026-10-07. Work used public data only; no purchasing, supplier contact or user questions. The arm is mains powered. There is no torso, mobile base or battery.

Choose two independent X lanes, with fixed OEM controllers outside the wet zone and one partitioned cable chain per lane. Passing means changing longitudinal order **on different lanes**. A carriage does not change lanes, and two carriages on the same lane cannot overtake. A three-lane/four-carriage cost-saving arrangement would therefore fail unrestricted reordering of the shared-lane pair. The four-carriage passing-rail budget below pays for four independent lanes; that expansion does not fit this galley.

The second choice adds a short Z slide to each lane, only if task IK shows a useful gain. This is a paired-guide heavy carriage with mechanical retention, not a small catalog belt module carrying a cantilevered cobot. Pass only with Z at its upper position. Complete process reach, especially the outer third arm, remains a task-team decision. The UR5e is the reused collision-model benchmark; it is not granted actuator or braking approval by this transport study.

The conventional industrial alternatives remain useful for transporting **containers**. Moving produce baskets or trays through a small product conveyor can give the kitchen much of the scheduling freedom without moving 25 kg arm bodies, large controllers or sharp tools through switches. One strong work arm plus task-specific lighter helpers is another worthwhile redesign: a 0.5–1 kg helper for a single leaf, small produce item or empty tray can have a lower payload requirement, but must be sized to its real fixture reach and gripper mass. Neither idea substitutes an untested appliance for the required finished products.

## Mass, force, moment and stiffness sizing

Use 25 kg arm body, 3 kg food payload, 1.2 kg additional gripper, 8 kg X carriage and 1 m full-reach envelope. UR5e has a shorter 850 mm catalog reach and 20.7 kg body, so the support sizing is deliberately broader. All arm mass and the food/gripper are conservatively placed at full reach rather than assuming an undocumented arm COM. The 3 kg is food at the tool; total flange load here is 4.2 kg, still subject to the chosen arm's load-COM curve. Specifications: [S21](https://www.universal-robots.com/manuals/EN/HTML/SW10_14/Content/prod-usr-man/complianceUR5e/H_g5_sections/appendix_g5/tech_spec_UR5e.htm).

For transport, Newtonian sizing uses `F = m a` and `M = Σ(r × F)`, with dynamic factor 1.5. At the full 60 kg design mass and 1 m/s² acceleration, inertial drive force is 90 N including the factor. Do not confuse that with 900 N vertical support capacity.

```
Fg = 1.5 × 9.81 × (25 + 3 + 1.2 + 8) = 547.40 N
Mg = 1.5 × 9.81 × [(25 + 3 + 1.2) × 1 + 8 × 0.15]
   = 447.34 N·m
Freference = sqrt(100² + 10² + 20²) = 102.47 N
Mgravity+process = 447.34 + 1.5 × 102.47 × 1 = 601.04 N·m
Selected support requirement: 60 kg, 900 N, 650 N·m about each axis
```

The 100/10/20 N case comes from `inputs/mandoline_golden.py`, and is retained as a conservative reference process envelope. No cutting, peeling or appliance force was invented. The current study changes transport hardware and one fixture's mounting orientation, not the cutting mechanism. Rail and Z motion are frozen during each reference process stroke. Any later powered dicer, washer or new tool needs its own physics/literature force and reaction-load evidence before it can replace those cases.

Four HGH25HA blocks are a sizing candidate. Each block is 48 × 40 × 104.6 mm, with catalog dynamic/static load ratings 42.20/69.07 kN and static moment ratings 560/730/730 N·m. Use two guide rows 340 mm apart and two blocks per row 240 mm apart. A conservative combined reaction bound is `900/4 + 650/(2×0.34) + 2×650/(2×0.24) = 3889.22 N/block`, below `C0/3 = 23023 N`. This is a preliminary load-distribution check, not a certified assembly moment rating or a fatigue-life calculation. [S01](https://www.hiwin.de/en/Products/Linear-guideways/Blocks/Ball-guides/Series-HG-QH/HGH-QHH/HGH25HA/p/HGH25HAZBC).

Two closed steel tubes per lane, each 150 mm deep in y × 50 mm in z × 3 mm wall, provide a 300 mm beam envelope. Physical track length is 2.4 m; the **end-supported structural span is 2.8 m**. With E=200 GPa and G=77 GPa engineering material assumptions, the combined `I=1.080584e-6 m⁴` and approximate closed-section `J=2.952636e-6 m⁴` give 1.90 mm vertical sag at 900 N and 0.115° twist at 650 N·m with rigid equal-sharing restraints. Twist contributes 2.00 mm at a 1 m TCP. These contributions leave little of the 5 mm system pose budget for guide, arch, frame, anchorage and arm deformation; the calculation does not grant the 5 mm/3° task acceptance. No slot is cut in the closed tubes for the cable chain.

Tube mass is 51.17 kg/lane, or 153.51 kg for three lanes, before guides, frame and guards. Conservative simultaneous wall-root moment bounds, including service moments, support-force stand-off and beam gravity but excluding frame weight, are **3.65 kN·m for two lanes and 6.57 kN·m for three**. The 40 mm portal sections in the clearance model are placeholders, not structural steel selections. A household wall/frame design cannot be accepted from these drawings alone.

## Power, loss-of-power restraint, hygiene and noise

The recommended chains work because each carriage stays on its own 2.4 m straight lane. Different lanes have independent horizontal-bending chains, each fed near x=-0.30 m. They do not cross, accumulate twist or travel through switches. OEM controllers remain fixed in dry, ventilated housing outside the work/splash volume, with mains, wired network and safety wiring fixed; OEM arm leads travel through their own chain. Do not reroute mains through an arbitrary arm connector or replace OEM safety wiring with Wi-Fi. A 6 m OEM lead is a length candidate for this travel; actual routing and strain-relief lengths remain to be confirmed.

The modeled chain has bend radius 110 mm, 245 mm swept width and 25 mm swept height. Current UR documentation lists 13.4/14.6 mm high-flex PUR cable options, while older manuals list 12.1 mm HiFlex. Cable generation and dynamic minimum radius must be matched. `110/14.6 = 7.53 D` is **not OEM approval**. The general IGUS cable-specific bend-radius examples cannot validate a UR cable; if the chosen OEM cable/chain requires more space, increase pitch/envelope and rerun. [S21](https://www.universal-robots.com/manuals/EN/HTML/SW10_14/Content/prod-usr-man/complianceUR5e/H_g5_sections/appendix_g5/tech_spec_UR5e.htm), [S26](https://toolbox.igus.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/chainflex-control-cables-catalog.pdf).

For truly free shuttles, evaluate enclosed conductor rails carrying rated arm-controller power plus PE, or isolated low-voltage DC only when the chosen OEM controller supports it. VAHLE KBH offers 4/5 poles and 63–200 A variants but IP23, or IP24 with strips, is not a washdown enclosure. Moving contacts need access prevention and collector-dust containment. A positive protective-earth path and supply interruption handling are mandatory for a mains controller. Electrical compatibility at transfer joints is not solved by drawing continuous red lines. [S16](https://www.vahle.com/fileadmin/cavok_upload/US%20Catalogs/vPOWER%2004b%20KBHF%20KBHS%20Enclosed%20Conductor%20System.pdf).

Inductive power is credible at arm-scale power, but carries mass, electronics and losses. VAHLE F150/RE150-LV is rated 1.5 kW at 10–80 V output with a 5 kg pickup; pickup IP54 and regulator IP20 still need housings. Some 4 kW peak units are only 1.2–1.6 kW rated, so peaks cannot be used as continuous supply capacity. It does not directly replace an AC OEM controller input. [S17](https://www.vahle.com/en/products/cps-140khz).

Beckhoff AT8200 NCT's 24 V, 35 W rated / 75 W peak is suitable for small grippers or sensors, not a general cobot supply; the 250 µs data feature does not change its power rating. VAHLE SMGM provides an Ethernet-interface microwave guide, with a 100 Mbit/s family, rather than sending raw EtherCAT through brush contacts. Deterministic timing, watchdog behavior and certified safety integration must be established for any such bridge. Wireless supervisory data is acceptable with local safety logic and a safe response to link loss; ordinary WLAN is not credited as a safety channel. [S09](https://www.beckhoff.com/en-us/products/motion/xts-linear-product-transport/at8x00-at9xxx-ath9xxx-xts-guide-rails-and-movers/at8200-2000-0100.html), [S18](https://www.vahle.com/fileadmin/cavok_upload/US%20Catalogs/vCOM%2006b%20SMGM%20SMGX%20Data%20Communication.pdf).

An OEM UR5e controller is 460 × 449 × 254 mm. Putting one plus a regulator on every switching carriage would materially change the backsplash geometry and moving mass. This packaging penalty is a major reason to prefer fixed controllers and chains; the free-transfer record in the JSON is topology only and not hardware-approved. [S21](https://www.universal-robots.com/manuals/EN/HTML/SW10_14/Content/prod-usr-man/complianceUR5e/H_g5_sections/appendix_g5/tech_spec_UR5e.htm).

Spring-applied, electrically released X brakes are required even for horizontal rails. Candidate BXR-050-10LE has 3.2 N·m holding torque. A 10:1 drive, 20 mm pinion radius and conservative 0.8 transmission factor give 1280 N static holding force. At 60 kg and 0.2 m/s, translational kinetic energy is 1.2 J; the catalog allows 200 J individual work and 40,000 J total work, but reflected rotor/drive inertia and brake wear still need allocation. The listed 60 ms engagement delay corresponds to 12 mm travel; a controlled 1 m/s² stop adds 20 mm, within the proposed 50 mm reserved end margin. This is a screening calculation, not a safety-rated stopping-distance guarantee. [S19](https://www.mikipulley.co.jp/en/electromagnetic-clutches-brakes/offbrakes/holding/bxr-le).

Use person detection, bounded 0.2 m/s empty X speed and 0.1 m/s loaded repositioning, and coordinated Category 1 / SS1 stops before energy removal. Apply spring brakes on loss of power; monitor brake release/application and holding ability. A motor holding brake does not by itself certify the whole stop. A Z slide also needs a direct spring restraint, such as a properly sized electrically released rod brake, and mechanical end stops; screw self-locking, gear friction and permanent magnets are not substitutes. The arm must meet the same spring-on-loss requirement on gravity-loaded axes. UR's public service description says each joint has a brake/friction clutch, but the sources inspected do not explicitly close the spring-mechanism proof. [S20](https://www.mayr.com/en/products/safety-brakes/industrial-brakes/roba-linearstop-electric~35072), [S22](https://www.universal-robots.com/manuals/EN/HTML/SW10_15/Content/prod-serv-man/E-series/serv-man-title-no-drive-pwr.htm).

Commercial SS1/STO capability, such as iTRAK's published SIL3/PLe, does not establish a mechanical spring brake on a carrier. ACOPOStrak's permanent magnets retain attachment to its guide, not independent longitudinal spring holding. FLOW6D advertises gentle landing/parking after power loss; that is not evidence of the required mechanical spring restraint under a 650 N·m cobot moment. ## Mounting and kitchen geometry

The model uses the authoritative T08 fixture dimensions, R01 fixed galley and conservative upper-cabinet/door/outlet envelopes. Coordinates are x along the counter, y=0 at the north counter front and y=+0.60 at the back wall, z=0 at the floor. Counter height is 0.85 m; opposite counter y=-1.8…-1.2. Actual household door hinges, faucet and sockets require a site survey.

Use an engineered reinforced-concrete connection, or an independent **floor-anchored** structural portal. Typical finish tile/plaster and hollow or aerated block are not structural rail anchors; hidden pipes/wires, substrate strength and waterproofing must be assessed before installation. Upper cabinets may hide a rail, but the cabinet carcass is not its load path. The above-cabinet end portal avoids the doors and faucet. Upper crossmembers occupy z=2.05…2.15 and stop at y=0.58, leaving 100 mm above cabinet tops and 20 mm before the wall. Wall pads are the only designated wall contact surfaces. Front end uprights at y=-0.425 clear the conservative door sweep by 40 mm. Intermediate wall brackets would cross carriage travel, so they are omitted; the beam is supported at its two ends.

### B — industrial 48 V DC sliding conductor rail / 滑触线

Use separate structural guides and insulated industrial copper conductors, with copper-graphite sliding shoes appropriate for the chosen **48 VDC** current and low-voltage contact behavior. Conductix 0811's short 40 mm copper-graphite collector is rated 40 A, versus 16 A for the pure-carbon version; each has 3 N normal preload and 0.09 kg mass. Tandem shoes improve continuity; **each remains capable of the whole 27.65 A steady budget** rather than assuming doubled capacity. Use an 80% continuous derating provision: 40 A→32 A. Catalog rating alone does not prove every brush grade at 48 VDC, temperature, condensation or startup duty. No lubrication on electrical contact surfaces. Inspect brush length, joint alignment, dust, contact voltage drop and heating; log travel distance. No unsupported kitchen replacement-km, arc-energy or service-year guarantee is given. Scraping/contact-bounce noise and PSU fans remain; no installed dBA estimate is asserted. Size a separate+48 V/0 V branch per lane; add a separately reliable PE/bonding path when the OEM/Class I arrangement requires it. The mechanical guide is not an acceptable unverified PE conductor. Do not keep re-energizing its controller at every seam.

**48 V is only the nominal voltage, not an established SELV system.** Establish protective separation, insulation, normal/single-fault voltage limits and the OEM controller's earth relationships. Floating protected output may qualify as SELV; earth-referenced 0 V is PELV. The exact GB/IEC classification for the completed cell must be established. It is a **price/capacity anchor, not a selected certified SELV assembly**. Select a suitable certified source, or validate an independent protective voltage-limit/disconnection system with the complete controller circuit. Thermal/current/short-circuit energy protections still matter at low voltage. [Mean Well RSP-1500 manufacturer specification](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/RSP-1500/RSP-1500-SPEC-CN.PDF).

Install a coordinated AC-side RCD and overload protection for the source/drive circuit. Type A is a candidate only where the actual converter leakage permits it; use suitable Type B/other approved detection where smooth DC leakage could blind upstream protection. Type A's published smooth-DC tolerance is 6 mA. **Do not place an ordinary AC Type B RCCB on the 48 V distribution bus:** its DC-leakage sensing capability does not make it a DC-network breaker. Protect DC branches with DC-rated devices; floating-output insulation monitoring and earth relationships must follow the selected safety design. [Schneider Type B RCCB applicability](https://www.se.com/th/en/faqs/FA339268/), [Schneider RCD coordination with DC leakage](https://www.electrical-installation.org/enwiki/RCDs_selection_in_presence_of_DC_earth_leakage_currents).

Use a local OEM controller and wired Ethernet to a carriage gateway; supervisory WLAN is possible with local execution/watchdogs and a separately certified safe-stop path. Safety functions must lead to Category 1/SS1 thenSTO on a commanded stop, followed by the specified spring-applied rail/arm/Z brakes. Abrupt power loss needs the brakes and a validated energy/UV strategy; sufficient SS 1-on-blackout hold-up is not established merely by a capacitor label. Ordinary Wi-Fi, household power-line networking or bare carbon brush poles do not qualify as a deterministic EtherCAT/safety channel. An industrial contactless Ethernet guide is another option, as discussed in the original report.

For passing rails, a continuous bus on each lane works but adds wear and controller packaging to a problem the present chains already solve. For a transfer shuttle, interlock rail locks and use manufacturer-compatible pickup guides, independently protected transfer-deck power, backfeed blocking and an energy reserve across its measured dead section. Never drag a live shoe through an open track joint or bridge unlike supplies. Parallel structural rails retain their original passing clearances; added bus/box/dust-guard geometry must be rebuilt and rerun with the same 10 mm/5 mm gap rules. There is no new faucet/outlet/cabinet-door exemption.

### C — dock-and-charge buffer carriage

C can cross dead switches without a running collector. Use industrial DC mating contacts and a mains-fed charger at stops; do not automate plugging a household 230 V twist adapter. Comparison includes a supercap and a battery because the addendum asks for both; the battery remains outside the adopted no-battery architecture.

Keep the onboard controller energized while traveling, rather than assuming the arm is an unpowered dead load. A 20 s travel/hold window is an engineering scenario, longer than the 9.5 s empty 1.9 m traverse at 0.2 m/s; it is not a measured washing-cycle duration. Using the UR steady provision 1045 W, 90% conversion, 1.5 energy margin and 80% end-of-life capacitance:

```text
E_required = 1045 W × 20 s × 1.5 / 0.90 = 34,833 J = 9.68 Wh
C_initial ≥ 2E / [(47.5² − 36²) × 0.80] = 90.69 F
Select at least 100 F initial guaranteed capacitance; measure real duty before shrinking.
```

47.5 V charging target gives headroom below a 48.6 V rated example; charger tolerance, transients and protective cutoff must remain within the module rating. 36 V is the assumed cutoff for this buffer scenario, within the cited OEM controller input ranges. At 36 V the provision demands 32.25 A. For xArm's 862 W provision, the same calculation needs 74.81 F. A tiny capacitor sized for contact bounce cannot be presented as a 20 s autonomous buffer.

Real buyable reference: Eaton XLR-48R6167-R has 166 F, 48.6 V rating, 5 mΩ maximum initial ESR, 86 A continuous at 15°C rise, and 80% end-of-life capacitance/200% ESR. Size using the larger datasheet 14.7 kg mass and approximately 419.5×194.5×177 mm envelope; the SKU's 14.197 kg differs. It provides 17.71 Wh from 47.5→36 V at end of life, before conversion/stop reserve; end-of-life ESR gives about 0.323 V drop at 32.25 A. This is a credible energy/current example but a poor fit for the small-carriage goal. Controller+module alone add 19 kg, and require new mass/inertia, moment, braking, stiffness and collision checks. Neither arm gravity brakes nor rail brakes may be bypassed because a buffer remains charged. [Eaton XLR-48 manufacturer datasheet](https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/products/electronic-components/resources/data-sheet/eaton-xlr-48-supercapacitor-module-data-sheet.pdf), [Eaton XLR-48 manufacturer SKU](https://www.eaton.com/ca/en-gb/skuPage.XLR-48R6167-R.html).

Price: 11,163.85 CNY per module on DigiKey China, including 13% VAT. The dock supply must support operating load **plus recharge**: reserve 2.5 kW output per lane, use a 3 kW supply example, not the 1.536 kW B supply. At 42 V, 2.5 kW is 59.52 A. A nominal 80 A contact class needs wire/bundling/temperature derating. With 1 kW net recharge, refilling the actual un-margined 20 s energy use takes at least 23.22 s ideally; regulation, balancing, conversion and taper increase that. Long washing/cutting holds require powered **task-station** docks, not just chargers at rail ends. [DigiKey China XLR-48 price](https://www.digikey.cn/zh/products/detail/eaton-electronics-division/XLR-48R6167-R/5956249).

CombiTac direqt's 4 mm family offers an 80 A class reference; its/10 power contacts are catalogued 81 A with 10 mm² conductors, with derating conditions. Bare fronts areIP 2X, which is touch protection, not water protection. Its catalogue gives 9.5 N average sliding force per contact and plugging force 1.5×sliding. Two power contacts therefore imply 28.5 N reference insertion; adding a separate 1.5 engineering margin gives 42.75 N. With an additional same-class PE contact the provision becomes 64.125 N. **These are not complete-dock peak-force ratings**: guide/seal, interlock and data-module forces and contaminated/end-of-life insertion still need sourced values or measurement. Have the carriage docking actuator bear the force, not the food-handling arm. [Staubli CombiTac direqt manufacturer catalog](https://www.staubli.com/content/dam/ecs/catalogs-brochures/IND/CT-Direqt-11014015-en.pdf).

The direqt family is rated up to 10,000 mating cycles; uniq offers up to 100,000 under its matched conditions. Do not transfer direqt force/current ratings to an unspecified uniq assembly. As an illustrative duty of 20 connections/day, 10,000 cycles is about 500 operating days versus 5000 days for 100,000, before environmental derating; these are arithmetic comparisons, not promised service lives. Matched housing ratingsIP 65/67 etc apply to specified closed assemblies, not any bare automatic dock while open. Supply a tested cover/drip guard for the open state and food-debris isolation. [Staubli CombiTac uniq manufacturer catalog](https://www.staubli.com/content/dam/ecs/catalogs-brochures/IND/CT-Main-11014009-en.pdf).

Mate/unmate atzero current; the manufacturer explicitly rejects mating under load. Use a keyed alignment/lock, last-mate-first-break verification, contactor/precharge and blocking of buffer backfeed, with protective earth first/last if required by the Class I design. Verify latched contacts before transferring load; isolate the buffer during maintenance and prevent automatic cutter/arm restart. DC-side short-circuit protection belongs close to **both** energy sources, along with balance/overvoltage/thermal monitoring. Commanded safety stops still require validated SS 1/STO and spring brakes, even if electronics stay powered. Regenerative braking energy cannot be pushed into a fully charged supercap or nonsinking source without a qualified absorption path. [Staubli CombiTac direqt manufacturer catalog](https://www.staubli.com/content/dam/ecs/catalogs-brochures/IND/CT-Direqt-11014015-en.pdf).

The battery comparison illustrates the power constraint: 9.68 Wh usable energy is small, but 32.25 A at 36 V is not. A conceptual 13-series high-rate P28A-type cell basis is 46.8 V nominal/54.6 V full, about 131 Wh and 0.598 kg cells only; 35 A is the published **cell maximum**, not certified pack continuous capability. It needs a qualified pack/BMS/charger/enclosure and thermal/current proof. This is not a pack build instruction or selected product. LYBATT's public 960 Wh, 25 A continuous/30 A peak, 37.5–54.75 V, 445×97×212 mm/IP64 pack has ample energy but the UR provision needs 30.96 A at 37.5 V, above both ratings. Its public matched price is unavailable. Smaller certified high-rate pack allowance 800–2000 CNY is a planning estimate, not a merchant quote. [Molicel P28A manufacturer cell datasheet](https://www.molicel.com/wp-content/uploads/INR18650P28A-V2-80093.pdf), [LYBATT Chinese manufacturer 48 V pack](https://cn.lybatt.com/products/48v-20ah-lifepo4-battery).

C data uses local execution/WLAN during transit, with an optional matched Ethernet docking module at rest and a separately validated safety link. Avoid making reacquisition of WLAN or Ethernet the sole stopping mechanism. Proposed transport-only subphases areD 00 controlled energization/precharge;D01 zero-current undock;D02 energy-bounded transit;D03 dock/latch verification;D04 zero-current connection/precharge/power transfer. They supplement allP 01–P16 finished-product requirements. They are not accepted task phases until docking forces/geometry and the unchanged checker pass; no new contacts/ACM exceptions were authorized. C permits mechanical stops anywhere for limited energized time; it does **not** provide indefinite powered work anywhere. Supercaps avoid sliding debris but add docking clicks, cooling and duty delays; no dBA measurements were found.
