**Mount height, localization and layout.** The upright base-joint gravity envelope is exactly zero: a vertical-axis joint experiences no gravity torque from the rated payload. Under the binding gravity-derived envelope rule, horizontal process demands therefore fail that denominator. A fixed 20° mount gives a nonzero base-axis envelope; no guessed motor torque was substituted. Both 0° and 20° envelopes were recomputed using two numerical restarts per joint and feasible OEM self-clear witnesses. These are conservative sampled maxima, not global-optimum proofs. [Envelope](../../sim/r09/integrator/assets/envelope_20.json).

The selected fixed mount is 850 mm. No lift is added because the observed blockers are fixture/food/gripper interference and ownership transitions, not evidence that vertical travel resolves the tasks. Base x/y/yaw is sampled at every start and frozen for each stroke. The planned docking accuracy is ±2 mm XY, ±0.2° yaw and ±1 mm height, using hard stops plus fiducials and applied wheel brakes. XY plus yaw at 850 mm reach already consumes about 5 mm before hand-eye error and compliance; this is a design requirement, not a measured positioning guarantee. Extra geometry reserve does not itself prove the 5 mm TCP pose tolerance under those errors.
