## Power track addendum

补充结论：家用轨道插座适合给固定电器和工装供电，放在经过确认的干燥位置。现有方案的机械臂仍采用各轨独立拖链；这样已经能错车、任意位置停靠，也能保留固定控制箱和有线通信。如果以后确实需要换轨，优先研究 **48 V 工业滑触线＋原厂直流控制箱**。家用旋转适配器不能充当带载滑动集电器。停靠充电只列为备选：按保持机械臂通电的保守工况计算，需要约100 F的电容储能；公开规格完整的166 F模块重14.7 kg，控制箱和护罩还会增加重量。电池方案按本次补充要求比较，但不纳入原来的无电池架构。

### Decision, evidence and power budgets

Use **A for fixed appliances**, retain **dedicated cable chains for the screened passing-arm layout**, and reserve **B for a future lane-changing design**. C is feasible as an electrical topology but is less simple for this kitchen. All comparisons use public data, with no purchase, account creation or supplier contact. Unknown ratings and prices remain explicitly unknown. Displayed China prices were inspected on 2026-10-07; indexed pages can be older snapshots, and none is a confirmed installation quote.

### A — household appliance/tool power track

A static aluminium/copper household track is useful for a stationary dicer, washer pump, spinner or tool charger. The adapter can be relocated between setups, then remains fixed while supplying power. Retain the OEM matching adapter/track/feed combination and certification; do not combine brands, field-cut or electrically splice a household track. The track carries electricity, **not the arm's 15–25 kg structural load or 650 N·m mounting moment**. Bull and Mijia have public product pages; a matched TOP track manual/IP/current price was not found, so an ordinary TOP extension-strip price is not substituted. [Bull official household power-track page](https://www.gongniu.cn/show-209-438-1.html), [Xiaomi official Mijia power-track product](https://www.mi.com/shop/buy/detail?product_id=19055).

The table lists 750 mm 431.85 and 1000 mm 559.53 CNY. It advertises 32 A/250 V/8000 W on the backbone. This retailer rating is not an OEM adapter or installed-IP certificate. A 2×1000 mm+500 mm buying example costs 1486.14 CNY using the 1000 mm table and 500 mm headline; it is **2.5 m purchased in separate modules serving about 2.4 m of counter**, not a cut-to-length continuous track. GQ1's recessed price is a budget anchor; a different surface/dry-panel SKU needs its own price. [Quanxingtai Bull GQ1 China retail listing](https://www.quanxingtai.com/goods.php?id=13131).

Apply the **lowest** track, adapter, feed, wire and protective-device rating. With a verified 10 A adapter at 220 V, its limit is 2200 W at unityPF; a 10 A feed supplies 2200 W total, and a 16 A feed 3520 W total. A 32 A backbone label does not upgrade the household circuit. For sizing illustration only, a 300 W dicer+100 W washer pump+150 W spinner totals 550 W/2.5 A at 220 V; replace these allowances with selected appliance running and starting-current datasheets. Cooking appliances remain outside this food-prep scope.

No matched GQ1/Mijia installed water-IP certificate was obtained: mark it **dry-only/IP unknown**, not IP44/IP65 by assumption. Site it in short dry segments beside stations with drip-protected entry, an accessible isolator, unobstructed wall outlets and cabinet doors, and no direct faucet/washer splash path. Do not prescribe a made-up universal sink setback; survey actual spray/reach. A canopy does not certify the track. Disconnect before cleaning; damp wipe accessible exteriors and avoid spraying into the slot. Aluminium coatings/contact surfaces need detergent compatibility; food-contact guards/trays use appropriate stainless steel or food-safe polymer. Electrical contacts are not food-contact components.

A 2023 consumer-commission test of 12 products found no aggregate overload/leakage protection in those tested units. Therefore do not assume an adapter's individual switch or a smart function provides upstream protection. Use a 30 mA RCBO as the design baseline for the AC appliance branch, verified protective earth for Class I appliances and metal housings, correct cable/overcurrent protection, and load-appropriate RCD type; the installation's earthing/certification still determines the final arrangement. [Consumer commission 2023 comparative track-socket test](https://www.hn315.net.cn/newones.asp?id=13340).

**Why it must not slide under load:** household twist/clip mating lacks an established continuous powered-sliding qualification, specified normal-force tolerance under rail misalignment, debris-management system and wear-distance rating. Engineering failure modes include contact bounce, scraped/oxidized copper, pitting, localI²R heating, broken PE sequencing and load arcing. DC interruptions are especially troublesome because there is no AC current zero. Manually de-energize, reposition and lock the adapter according to its OEM instructions; do not attach it to a motor-driven arm carriage. The household product's convenient repositioning is not evidence that energized scraping is rated. This is an engineering rejection from missing duty evidence, not a claim of a verified manufacturer ban for every household SKU.

Appliance station control uses fixed wired I/O; WLAN/smart adapters may offer supervisory switching but receive no safety credit and must not auto-restart a cutter after power restoration. A neither obstructs arm passing nor supplies arms through switches: it is stationary utility infrastructure. It has no sliding noise; no matched dBA measurement was found.

### B — industrial 48 V DC sliding conductor rail / 滑触线

Use separate structural guides and insulated industrial copper conductors, with copper-graphite sliding shoes appropriate for the chosen **48 VDC** current and low-voltage contact behavior. Conductix 0811's short 40 mm copper-graphite collector is rated 40 A, versus 16 A for the pure-carbon version; each has 3 N normal preload and 0.09 kg mass. Four power shoes per carriage—tandem on+48 V and tandem onreturn—have 12 N total normal force and 0.36 kg shoe mass before mounts/leads. Tandem shoes improve continuity; **each remains capable of the whole 27.65 A steady budget** rather than assuming doubled capacity. Use an 80% continuous derating provision: 40 A→32 A. Catalog rating alone does not prove every brush grade at 48 VDC, temperature, condensation or startup duty. [Conductix-Wampfler 0811 German manufacturer catalog](https://www.conductix.com/sites/default/files/downloads/KAT0811-0002-D_Isolierte_Schleifleitung_SingleFlexLine_Programm_0811_1.pdf).

The 3 N is a sourced normal contact force. Tangential drag must be measured or supported by a matched carbon/copper friction source; the unchangedμ=0.5 gripper coefficient does not apply to it. No fabricated collector friction force, cooking force or contact exemption was inserted into the simulation.

Conductix 0811 is anIP 23 installed-system family; VAHLE KBH listsIP 23/IP24 variants with seals. These do not establish washdown, grease exclusion or food-safe wear debris. Use a guarded dry channel with accessible isolated maintenance, down/lateral protected pickup opening and a removable carbon/copper-dust catch tray separated from produce and bowls. No lubrication on electrical contact surfaces. Inspect brush length, joint alignment, dust, contact voltage drop and heating; log travel distance. No unsupported kitchen replacement-km, arc-energy or service-year guarantee is given. Scraping/contact-bounce noise and PSU fans remain; no installed dBA estimate is asserted. [Conductix-Wampfler 0811 manufacturer Chinese catalog](https://cdn.conductix.com.cn/cdn/ff/19EmMw97qmKO53e4ZdeTTQlzXvn-F2gwMQyq7ULb3kY/1753690205/public/downloads/KAT0811-0002I-CN%20%E5%8D%95%E6%9E%81%E7%BB%84%E5%90%88%E5%BC%8F%E6%BB%91%E8%A7%A6%E7%BA%BFSingleFlexLine0811.pdf), [VAHLE KBHF/KBHS manufacturer catalog](https://www.vahle.com/fileadmin/cavok_upload/US%20Catalogs/vPOWER%2004b%20KBHF%20KBHS%20Enclosed%20Conductor%20System.pdf).

Size a separate+48 V/0 V branch per lane; add a separately reliable PE/bonding path when the OEM/Class I arrangement requires it. The mechanical guide is not an acceptable unverified PE conductor. Keep polarity correct, guard all live pickup faces, and prevent collector backfeed between supplies. A limited ride-through capacitor can suppress **measured** bounce or switch gaps; its required duration must include the verified stop-energy path. DC-rated local fuses/breakers and an actual OEM-compatible startup/precharge interface are required. 40 A collector ratings do not cover UR's up-to 400 A energization surge. Do not keep re-energizing its controller at every seam.

**48 V is only the nominal voltage, not an established SELV system.** Establish protective separation, insulation, normal/single-fault voltage limits and the OEM controller's earth relationships. Floating protected output may qualify as SELV; earth-referenced 0 V is PELV. IEC61010-2-201's control-equipment preview gives 60 VDC dry and 35 VDC wet definitions; this illustrates why accessible wet 48 V contacts should not be called touch-safe. The exact GB/IEC classification for the completed cell must be established. Put the protected 48 V path outside wet access, use enclosed contacts, and use 24 V or a suitably qualified lower-voltage interface where accessible wet contacts are unavoidable. [IEC-authored control-equipment standard preview](https://www.iteh.us/catalog/standards/clc/6caddc76-f5f1-4ede-a4a8-aa90a4c53602/en-iec-61010-2-201-2024).

The pricedRSP-1500-48 has an overvoltage-trip range 57.6–67.2 V; that upper value prevents claiming a ≤60 V single-fault limit from its 48 V label alone. It is a **price/capacity anchor, not a selected certified SELV assembly**. Select a suitable certified source, or validate an independent protective voltage-limit/disconnection system with the complete controller circuit. Thermal/current/short-circuit energy protections still matter at low voltage. [Mean Well RSP-1500 manufacturer specification](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/RSP-1500/RSP-1500-SPEC-CN.PDF).

Install a coordinated AC-side RCD and overload protection for the source/drive circuit. Type A is a candidate only where the actual converter leakage permits it; use suitable Type B/other approved detection where smooth DC leakage could blind upstream protection. Type A's published smooth-DC tolerance is 6 mA. **Do not place an ordinary AC Type B RCCB on the 48 V distribution bus:** its DC-leakage sensing capability does not make it a DC-network breaker. Protect DC branches with DC-rated devices; floating-output insulation monitoring and earth relationships must follow the selected safety design. [Schneider Type B RCCB applicability](https://www.se.com/th/en/faqs/FA339268/), [Schneider RCD coordination with DC leakage](https://www.electrical-installation.org/enwiki/RCDs_selection_in_presence_of_DC_earth_leakage_currents).

Use a local OEM controller and wired Ethernet to a carriage gateway; supervisory WLAN is possible with local execution/watchdogs and a separately certified safe-stop path. Safety functions must lead to Category 1/SS1 thenSTO on a commanded stop, followed by the specified spring-applied rail/arm/Z brakes. Abrupt power loss needs the brakes and a validated energy/UV strategy; sufficient SS 1-on-blackout hold-up is not established merely by a capacitor label. Ordinary Wi-Fi, household power-line networking or bare carbon brush poles do not qualify as a deterministic EtherCAT/safety channel. An industrial contactless Ethernet guide is another option, as discussed in the original report.

For passing rails, a continuous bus on each lane works but adds wear and controller packaging to a problem the present chains already solve. For a transfer shuttle, interlock rail locks and use manufacturer-compatible pickup guides, independently protected transfer-deck power, backfeed blocking and an energy reserve across its measured dead section. Never drag a live shoe through an open track joint or bridge unlike supplies. Parallel structural rails retain their original passing clearances; added bus/box/dust-guard geometry must be rebuilt and rerun with the same 10 mm/5 mm gap rules. There is no new faucet/outlet/cabinet-door exemption.

### C — dock-and-charge buffer carriage

C can cross dead switches without a running collector. Use industrial DC mating contacts and a mains-fed charger at stops; do not automate plugging a household 230 V twist adapter. Comparison includes a supercap and a battery because the addendum asks for both; the battery remains outside the adopted no-battery architecture.

Keep the onboard controller energized while traveling, rather than assuming the arm is an unpowered dead load. A 20 s travel/hold window is an engineering scenario, longer than the 9.5 s empty 1.9 m traverse at 0.2 m/s; it is not a measured washing-cycle duration. Using the UR steady provision 1045 W, 90% conversion, 1.5 energy margin and 80% end-of-life capacitance:

```text
E_required = 1045 W × 20 s × 1.5 / 0.90 = 34,833 J = 9.68 Wh
C_initial ≥ 2E / [(47.5² − 36²) × 0.80] = 90.69 F
Select at least 100 F initial guaranteed capacitance; measure real duty before shrinking.
```

47.5 V charging target gives headroom below a 48.6 V rated example; charger tolerance, transients and protective cutoff must remain within the module rating. 36 V is the assumed cutoff for this buffer scenario, within the cited OEM controller input ranges. At 36 V the provision demands 32.25 A. For xArm's 862 W provision, the same calculation needs 74.81 F. A tiny capacitor sized for contact bounce cannot be presented as a 20 s autonomous buffer.

Real buyable reference: Eaton XLR-48R6167-R has 166 F, 48.6 V rating, 5 mΩ maximum initial ESR, 86 A continuous at 15°C rise, and 80% end-of-life capacitance/200% ESR. Size using the larger datasheet 14.7 kg mass and approximately 419.5×194.5×177 mm envelope; the SKU's 14.197 kg differs. It provides 17.71 Wh from 47.5→36 V at end of life, before conversion/stop reserve; end-of-life ESR gives about 0.323 V drop at 32.25 A. This is a credible energy/current example but a poor fit for the small-carriage goal. Controller+module alone add 19 kg, and require new mass/inertia, moment, braking, stiffness and collision checks. Neither arm gravity brakes nor rail brakes may be bypassed because a buffer remains charged. [Eaton XLR-48 manufacturer datasheet](https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/products/electronic-components/resources/data-sheet/eaton-xlr-48-supercapacitor-module-data-sheet.pdf), [Eaton XLR-48 manufacturer SKU](https://www.eaton.com/ca/en-gb/skuPage.XLR-48R6167-R.html).

Price: 11,163.85 CNY per module on DigiKey China, including 13% VAT. A nominal 80 A contact class needs wire/bundling/temperature derating. With 1 kW net recharge, refilling the actual un-margined 20 s energy use takes at least 23.22 s ideally; regulation, balancing, conversion and taper increase that. Long washing/cutting holds require powered **task-station** docks, not just chargers at rail ends. [DigiKey China XLR-48 price](https://www.digikey.cn/zh/products/detail/eaton-electronics-division/XLR-48R6167-R/5956249).

CombiTac direqt's 4 mm family offers an 80 A class reference; its/10 power contacts are catalogued 81 A with 10 mm² conductors, with derating conditions. Use a keyed alignment/lock, last-mate-first-break verification, contactor/precharge and blocking of buffer backfeed, with protective earth first/last if required by the Class I design. [Staubli CombiTac direqt manufacturer catalog](https://www.staubli.com/content/dam/ecs/catalogs-brochures/IND/CT-Direqt-11014015-en.pdf).
