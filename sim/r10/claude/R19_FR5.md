## 4. Adapter mass and payload (`r19fr5_payload.py`, `results/r19/fr5/payload_fr5_j6_pgc_sp20.json`)

### 4.1 Adapter design and mass
- **Robot face:** FR5 end flange [S, Fairino Cobots manual §3.4.2, Fig. 3.4-13, https://fairino-doc-en.readthedocs.io/latest/CobotsManual/installation.html]:
  - Ø63h7;
  - 4 × M6-6H, 12.5 deep, on a Ø50 circle (45° off the pin);
  - Ø6H7 pin hole, 6 deep;
  - Ø31.5H7 centring recess, 6.2 deep;
  - M6 at 8 N·m, grade ≥ 8.8.
  - That is ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6.
- **Gripper face:** PGC-140 [S, DH PGC-140 Short Manual v2.1, §1.4 and Table 1.4, https://uploads.unchainedrobotics.de/media/file_upload/PGC-140-Short-Manual_v2.1_e46a3c6b.pdf]:
  - DH's standard flange follows ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6;
  - the kit fixes the gripper with 4 × M4×6 thin-head screws and 2 × Ø3×6 pins, and the robot side with 4 × M6×12 and a Ø6×10 pin;
  - the M4 circle diameter is only in the drawing [not read; E].
  - Body Ø75 (DH drawing, as `arms.py` `oem_source`).
- **The part:** a 6061 disc Ø75 × 20 mm.
  - Robot side: a Ø31.5 g6 × 6 mm spigot into the FR5 recess, a Ø6 pin hole, and 4 × Ø6.6 holes with Ø11 × 6.5 counterbores on Ø50 for M6×20 screws.
  - Gripper side: 4 × M4 and 2 × Ø3 pin holes.
  - It replaces DH's standard flange, so it is one plate, 20 mm longer.
- **Mass [E]:**
  - volume 88.4 (disc) + 4.7 (spigot) − 4.3 (M6 holes) − 0.5 (M4 and pin holes) = 88.2 cm³;
  - × 2.70 g/cm³ (aluminium, handbook) = **238 g**, + ≈ 8 g longer screws, so **≈ 0.25 kg**;
  - a Ø63 disc would be ≈ 0.18 kg, a pocketed one ≈ 0.12 kg.
  - CoG on the flange axis, about 10 mm out.
- **Not modelled before either:** DH's standard flange (≈ 0.05–0.10 kg [E]), which this plate replaces. The modelled EOAT (1.0 kg PGC [S, Table 1.1] + 0.2 kg fingers) does not include it.

### 4.2 Payload against the FR5 load curve
- **Rule** [S, Fairino manual §3.4.5.2.5, Fig. 3.4-19, same URL]:
  - rated 5 kg (maximum 7 kg) at **full performance up to 300 mm CoG offset**, measured from the flange centre;
  - beyond 300 mm the curve read off the figure falls about as constant moment: ≈ 4.0 kg at 400 mm, 3.25 kg at 500 mm, 2.5 kg at 640 mm [S, read off the curve].
- **The PGC limits** [S]: 7 N·m static Mx / My / Mz and 300 N static vertical (Table 1.2); recommended workpiece 3 kg (https://www.welectron.com/DH-Robotics-PGC-140-50-Collaborative-Electric-Parallel-Gripper).
- **How it is computed:** the held load is rigid in the TCP frame, so its CoG offset from the flange is a constant of the grasp [M].
  - Its CoG relative to the TCP is computed from the stored grasp: the wok from the `sp20` wok chain; the tub from the UR5e tub-fill chain, since no FR5 tub chain exists, and the grasp is the same.
  - It is then composed with the model's flange→TCP transform: TCP at (0, 13.4, 147.9) mm with the adapter, 127.9 mm without.
  - Added to it: the modelled EOAT (1.2 kg) and the adapter (0.25 kg at 10 mm).

| Load | Held | Payload no adapter → **sp20** | CoG offset from flange | Margin to 5 kg | PGC jaw moment (limit 7 N·m) | Static flange moment, worst orientation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wok + heaviest dish (1.5 + 0.3 + 1.15 kg) | 2.95 kg | 4.15 → **4.40 kg** | 157 → **158 mm** (axial 90, radial 131) | **0.60 kg** | **6.23 N·m** level (6.47 worst orientation); change **0** | 6.40 → 6.84 N·m |
| Full soak tub (0.50 + 2.28 kg) | 2.78 kg | 3.98 → **4.23 kg** | 213 → **219 mm** (axial 212) | **0.77 kg** | **4.83 N·m**; change **0** | 8.32 → 9.10 N·m |

- **Both are inside the full-performance envelope** (≤ 5 kg, ≤ 300 mm).
  - At 4.40 kg the curve allows ≈ 360 mm [S read off], so the wok has ≈ 200 mm of offset reserve.
  - The tub's held CoG alone is 303 mm from the flange with the adapter (284 mm without). The gripper's and adapter's mass, near the flange, pull the combined CoG in to 219 mm.
- **The adapter adds:**
  - 0.25 kg of payload;
  - +20 mm along the tool axis to everything below it;
  - at most +0.44 N·m (wok) and +0.78 N·m (tub) static moment at the flange.
- **The PGC jaw moment does not change** with the adapter: the held object's pose relative to the jaws is the same.
  - The wok stays the binding item at 6.23 N·m of 7, as before (README: 6.3 N·m). It is also at DH's 3 kg recommended workpiece (2.95 kg).
- **Sensitivity [E]:**
  - a Ø75 adapter with no lightening (0.26 kg) plus the cable's first section changes the margins by < 0.05 kg;
  - keeping DH's standard flange **and** adding a separate 20 mm spacer would cost about 0.1 kg more: wok margin ≈ 0.5 kg.
