# Cooking skills Claude can call

Generated by `cook_api.write_api_md()`.

Routines (predefined sequences; they run straight through without waiting for you):
- mise_en_place(tomatoes: number [0, 2], cups: items 6, drum: items 4) [sum of the steps] — Everything before the heat goes on, in one go: fetch the cold box, cut `tomatoes` tomatoes, dose each of `cups` into its own robot cup, and load `drum` (up to 4 compartments, in release order).
- heat_to(level: number [0.1, 1], target_C: number [40, 200]) [until target] — Heat at `level` until the thermometer reads `target_C` (needs oil or food in the wok), at most 120 s; stops early if the reading passes 200 °C.
- add_off_heat(source: string, level: number [0, 1]) [~10 s] — Heat off (plume rule), pour `source` into the wok, then set the heat back to `level` (0 = leave it off).
- fry_still(seconds: number [3, 120]) [seconds] — Leave the food to fry undisturbed for `seconds` at the current heat (heat must be ≤ 0.5).
- flip_and_fry(seconds: number [0, 120]) [4 s + seconds] — Turn the egg over, then fry the second side undisturbed for `seconds`.
- stir_fry(level: number [0, 1], seconds: number [3.6, 90]) [seconds] — Set the heat to `level`, then stir for `seconds` (takes the spatula if needed).
- drum_add_stir(seconds: number [3.6, 90]) [1.5 s + seconds] — Release the next drum compartment and stir straight after it for `seconds` (no pause). Use for ketchup, water, starch water, finishing oil.
- take_out(into: enum ["plate", "holding_bowl", "oil_cup"]) [as tip_wok] — Heat off, tip everything in the wok into `into` (holding_bowl, oil_cup or plate).
- return_and_toss(level: number [0, 1], seconds: number [3.6, 60]) [~10 s + seconds] — Heat off, pour the holding bowl back in, heat at `level` and stir for `seconds`.
Single skills (for anything the routines do not cover):
- fridge_fetch() [25 s] — Open the fridge, take the robot cold box from the door bin (the user loaded today's cold items: 3 beaten eggs in a cup, 150 g, 5 °C), set it on the counter, close the door. (not verified in sim: the eggs are cracked and beaten by the user beforehand (one gripper cannot crack eggs))
- prep_tomato() [45 s per tomato] — Pick one tomato from the raw tray, rinse it under the tap, cut it into 8 wedges on the lever cutter (about 150 g of wedges into the tomato bowl). (not verified in sim: the replay starts with the tomato already gripped; rinse water and the cutting itself are not physically simulated; 45 s is an estimate)
- dose_cup(what: enum ["oil", "water", "starch_water", "ketchup", "salt", "sugar", "finishing_oil"], grams: number [0.5, 300]) [10 s] — The seasoning station doses one item by weight into a robot cup and the arm takes it.
- pour_into_wok(source: string) [8 s] — Arm pours a source into the wok: a cup id ('cup1'…), 'egg', 'tomato' or 'holding_bowl'. The arm is over the wok, so the heat must be 0 (plume rule).
- load_drum(items: items 4) [15 s + 5 s per compartment] — Station fills the 4-compartment pour drum (in this order) and the arm drops it into the servo holder behind the wok. Heat must be 0. Later drum_release() empties one compartment at a time, with the heat on and without the arm. (not verified in sim: holder spot behind the wok has zero clearance reserve; liquids not simulated)
- drum_release(stir_seconds: number [0, 60]) [1.5 s (+ stir_seconds)] — Servo rolls the drum to empty its next compartment into the wok. The arm is not needed. stir_seconds > 0 makes it one movement set: the arm (taking the spatula first if needed) stirs straight after the release, with no pause for a decision; use it for anything that must not sit unstirred.
- set_heat(level: number [0, 1]) [1 s] — Set the induction cooker power: 0 = off (离火), 0.3 ≈ low, 0.5 ≈ medium, 1.0 = full (2975 W into the wok).
- stir(seconds: number [3.6, 90]) [seconds (+27.4 s to take the spatula)] — Stir-fry with the arm-held spatula (scrape strokes, about 3.6 s each). If the hand is free, the arm first takes the spatula from its rest on the counter left of the sink (27.4 s). Any skill that needs the arm (pour, dose, prep, fridge, drum loading, wok tip) first puts the spatula back (27.4 s); the cooker keeps running meanwhile. drum_release, set_heat, look and wait do not need the arm.
- flip_egg() [4 s] — Slide the spatula under the egg and turn it over. (not verified in sim: uses the scrape stroke; flipping a single piece was not simulated)
- tip_wok(into: enum ["plate", "holding_bowl", "oil_cup"]) [plate 79.5 s, holding_bowl / oil_cup 27 s] — Heat must be 0. Lift the wok off the cooker, carry it to the counter left of the hood and tip it toward the robot to 90°. 'plate' = serve: tip onto the plate, then rinse the wok at the sink under the running faucet and set it back (ends the dish). 'holding_bowl' (take food out to add it back later) or 'oil_cup' (润锅 oil pour-out) = tip at the second spot and set the wok straight back, no rinse. (not verified in sim: holding_bowl / oil_cup: the return to the cooker skips the sink (lift path reversed, not re-run); tipping into a cup (narrower than a plate) not simulated)
- look() [1 s] — Take a wok-camera image and read the thermometer.
- wait(seconds: number [1, 120]) [seconds] — Do nothing for a while (the cooker keeps running). With food in the wok and heat > 0.5, pauses longer than 6 s are refused (R15 half-power policy).
- done(success: boolean, summary: string) [0 s] — Finish and report.
