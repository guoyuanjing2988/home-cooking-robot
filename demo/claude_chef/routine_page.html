Cooking routines

A routine is a predefined sequence of robot steps that runs straight through, with stop rules from the thermometer and the clock. Claude chooses which routine runs and with what settings. It never waits mid-routine for a decision, and it never invents a motion.

Why routines. In a real-time run (run04) the stove kept going while Claude thought, about 10 s per decision. Claude released the starch water, then stopped to think before stirring, and the hidden food model logged lumps 9 s later.

t=865 s drum_release (starch water) · 10 s decision · t=874 s starch_lumps: thickened without stirring

"Release and stir" is now one routine. A real kitchen needs hundreds of them, extracted from recipes and verified one by one (issue #32).
